# Demo narration (not the lab's script)

## Slide 1 — Cells and wires

Six cells sit on a chip. Each group of wires has a box around its cells. Add the width and the height of every box, and you get the wirelength: fifty-two.

## Slide 2 — One move

The annealer picks one cell and nudges it. Cell D goes right and hits the wall. The wires get three longer. At temperature three, the chance to accept is thirty-seven percent. The dice says zero point one seven, so the move is accepted.

## Slide 3 — Hot to cold

Now watch all sixty moves. The temperature falls from three to almost zero. Forty-four moves are accepted, sixteen are rejected. The best wirelength is forty-nine point six.

## Slide 4 — One seed, one story

Another seed tells another story. Seed one hundred ninety-nine never beats the start. Over two hundred seeds, the best goes from thirteen point seven to fifty-two.

## Slide 5 — Your turn

With three hundred moves, the wires shrink to two point six. Is that a good placement?
